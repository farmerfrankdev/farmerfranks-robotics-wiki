# Transporter

[Home](README.md) · [Robots](Robots.md)

![Transporter](images/robot.png)

The Transporter carries items from one chest to another. It takes a stack of one kind of item at a time from its pickup chest and walks it over to its delivery chest. It's the plain white robot with a pink antenna bulb.

## Recipe
Build it at the [Robot Workshop](Robot-Workshop.md) from a Robot Frame and a **Transporter Neural Chip**.

![Transporter Neural Chip recipe](images/recipes/transporter_chip.png)

## Setup
With the [Robot Linker](Robot-Linker.md), click:
1. the chest to fetch from;
2. the chest to deliver to;
3. its charging station.

## Tips
- A [Capacity Upgrade](Upgrades.md) lets it carry two stacks, one in each hand.
- A [Filter Chip](Filter-Chips.md) decides which items it carries.
- Uses the normal amount of energy (1×).
