# Killerbot

[Home](README.md) · [Robots](Robots.md)

![Killerbot](images/robot_killer.png)

The Killerbot hunts hostile mobs in its area. When it reaches them it spins round with a sword in each hand, hitting every mob around it. Drops are left on the ground. It's the dark gunmetal robot with a purple stripe.

Animals, villagers and players are left alone, and no robot is ever targeted or hurt by mobs.

## Recipe
Build it at the [Robot Workshop](Robot-Workshop.md) from a Robot Frame and a **Killer Neural Chip**.

![Killer Neural Chip recipe](images/recipes/killer_chip.png)

## Setup
With the [Robot Linker](Robot-Linker.md), click:
1. two corners on the ground for its area;
2. its charging station.

It does not use a chest.

## Tips
- A [Combat Upgrade](Upgrades.md) gives it diamond swords that reach 4 blocks instead of 2 and hit twice as hard.
- A [Filter Chip](Filter-Chips.md) with spawn eggs picks which mobs it hunts.
- Put a [Collector](Collector.md) in the same area for the drops, and a [Robot Revolving Door](Robot-Revolving-Door.md) in the wall, so the mob farm stays dark.
- Uses four times the normal amount of energy (4×).
