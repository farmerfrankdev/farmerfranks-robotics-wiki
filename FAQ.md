# FAQ

[Home](README.md)

### My robot just stands there.
Right-click it with an empty hand, or with the [Inspector](Inspector.md). It tells you what it is doing and what it still needs. The usual reasons are:
- **It is not set up.** Set it up with the [Robot Linker](Robot-Linker.md), and remember to right-click the robot at the end to hand it the setup.
- **It has no power.** Its eyes are dark and it slumps over. Check that its [Charging Station](Charging-Station.md) has energy.
- **It has nothing to do.** For example, the pickup chest is empty, no crops are ripe, or there is no water in a Fisher's area.
- **This kind of robot is turned off** in the server's [config](Configuration.md).

### The linker says the area is too big.
Areas can be up to 32 × 32 blocks, or 48 × 48 with a [Range Upgrade](Upgrades.md).

### Can robots get stuck in trees or walls?
Robots that end up inside a block find a free spot nearby and hop out.

### How do I move a robot?
Shift + right-click it with an empty hand. It goes into your inventory with its charge, modules and setup. Right-click a block to put it down again.

### Can animals or mobs get out through the robots' door?
No. The [Robot Revolving Door](Robot-Revolving-Door.md) is a wall for everything but robots, and lets no light through.

### Does it work with other mods' pipes and cables?
Yes. Generators, batteries, the Charging Station and the Robot Workshop all use standard FE, and the machines accept hoppers and item pipes through their sides.

### Is there a version for an older Minecraft version, or for Fabric?
Not yet. The mod is made for Minecraft 26.3 on NeoForge. Older versions may come if many people ask.

### Can I use it in my modpack?
Yes. See [Modpack Makers](Modpack-Makers.md).
