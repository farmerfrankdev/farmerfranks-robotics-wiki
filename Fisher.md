# Fisher

[Home](README.md) · [Robots](Robots.md)

![Fisher](images/robot_fisher.png)

The Fisher walks to the edge of the water in its area, casts its rod and waits 5 to 30 seconds for a bite, just like a player fishing. The catch comes from the vanilla fishing loot: mostly fish, some junk, and now and then treasure. It jumps for joy over every catch. It wears a yellow raincoat.

## Recipe
Build it at the [Robot Workshop](Robot-Workshop.md) from a Robot Frame and a **Fisher Neural Chip**.

![Fisher Neural Chip recipe](images/recipes/fisher_chip.png)

## Setup
With the [Robot Linker](Robot-Linker.md), click:
1. two corners on the ground for its area, with water in it;
2. the chest to deliver to;
3. its charging station.

## Tips
- It delivers its basket (4 stacks, or 8 with a [Capacity Upgrade](Upgrades.md)) when it is full, or after a minute.
- A [Speed Upgrade](Upgrades.md) also makes it get bites sooner.
- A [Filter](Filter-Chips.md) decides which catches it keeps. The rest go back into the water.
- Uses the normal amount of energy (1×).
