# Harvester

[Home](README.md) · [Robots](Robots.md)

![Harvester](images/robot_harvester.png)

The Harvester harvests ripe crops in its area and delivers the harvest to a chest. It handles wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart, and pumpkins and melons grown on a stem. The farmland is left as it is, so a Planter can sow it again. It's the pumpkin-orange robot.

## Recipe
Build it at the [Robot Workshop](Robot-Workshop.md) from a Robot Frame and a **Harvester Neural Chip**.

![Harvester Neural Chip recipe](images/recipes/harvester_chip.png)

## Setup
With the [Robot Linker](Robot-Linker.md), click:
1. two corners on the ground for its area;
2. the chest to deliver to;
3. its charging station.

## Tips
- It carries up to 4 stacks at a time, or 8 with a [Capacity Upgrade](Upgrades.md).
- A [Filter](Filter-Chips.md) with a crop (such as wheat) or its seeds picks which crops it harvests.
- Pair it with a [Planter](Planter.md) for a farm that runs itself.
- Uses the normal amount of energy (1×).
